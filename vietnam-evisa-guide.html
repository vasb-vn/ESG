Overview

What is the Vietnam E-Visa?

Vietnam's Electronic Visa (E-Visa) is an official entry document issued online by the Vietnam Immigration Department (Ministry of Public Security). It replaces the need to visit a Vietnamese Embassy in person.

Since 2023, the E-Visa is available to citizens of all countries and territories worldwide, with a single online application and a processing time of 3 to 6 working days.

Documents Required

What You Need to Prepare

  • 1
    Valid Passport

    Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned date of entry into Vietnam. Must have at least 1 blank page for the entry stamp at the border.

  • 2
    Scanned Passport Bio Page (Data Page)

    A clear scan of the page showing your photo, full name, date of birth, passport number, and nationality. File must be in JPG or JPEG format, under 2 MB. The full page must be visible — no cropped edges or glare.

  • 3
    Passport-Style Portrait Photo

    A recent photo meeting all the following specifications:

    🧑
    • Size: 4 × 6 cm
    • Format: JPG or JPEG, under 2 MB
    • White or light plain background
    • Face directly forward, both eyes open
    • Neutral expression — do not smile
    • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
    • No glasses or tinted lenses
    • Photo must be taken within the last 6 months
  • 4
    Payment Method

    An international credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, or equivalent) to pay the visa fee online during your application.

  • 5
    Valid Email Address

    You will receive your Application Code and all status notifications by email. Ensure the email address you enter is accessible and monitored.

Application Process

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Access the Official Portal

Open a web browser and go to the official Vietnam E-Visa website. Click the button "Apply Now" (for foreign nationals applying individually from outside Vietnam).

🌐 https://evisa.gov.vn
2

Read & Accept the Declaration

Read the application instructions on screen carefully. Scroll to the bottom, tick both confirmation checkboxes, then click "Next" to proceed.

3

Upload Your Photos

Upload your portrait photo and your passport bio page scan as separate files. The system will attempt to auto-read your passport details — review them carefully and correct any errors.

💡 Tip: Make sure both images are clear, well-lit, and unobstructed. Low-quality photos are the most common cause of application delays.
4

Complete the Application Form

Fill in all required fields (marked with *). Key information to enter:

Full name: Enter exactly as it appears in your passport (First name → Middle name → Last name order).

Intended entry/exit ports: Select the border gate(s) you plan to use. You must enter and exit through officially approved E-Visa ports only.

Visa type: Select Single Entry (US$25) or Multiple Entry (US$50) based on your travel plans.

5

Review & Confirm

Review all information for accuracy before submitting. Errors in your name, passport number, or travel dates may cause rejection — and fees are non-refundable.

⚠️ Name must match your passport exactly, including middle names and diacritics.
6

Pay the Visa Fee Online

Proceed to the secure payment gateway and pay using an international credit or debit card. Upon successful payment, you will receive an Application Code by email. Save this code — you will need it to check your status.

7

Wait for Processing

Under normal circumstances, applications are processed within 3 to 6 working days. Vietnamese public holidays may add to the processing time — plan accordingly.

8

Check Your Application Status

Track your application at the link below using your Application Code, email address, and date of birth.

🔍 https://evisa.gov.vn/e-visa/search

When the status shows "Granted", download and save the PDF of your approved e-visa.

9

Print Your E-Visa & Travel

Print a physical copy of your approved e-visa document. At the port of entry, present the printed e-visa together with your original passport to the immigration officer.

🖨️ Keep both a printed copy and a digital copy on your phone as backup.
Important Notes

Before You Travel

  • ✈️
    The E-Visa is accepted at 83 designated ports of entry across Vietnam as of December 2025, including all major international airports (Noi Bai – Hanoi, Tan Son Nhat – Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang), most international land border crossings, and seaports. Confirm your planned entry point is on the approved list before booking travel.
  • 📅
    The E-Visa validity period begins on the start date you enter in the application, not the date of arrival. You may enter on any day within the validity window. If your start date passes before you enter, the visa cannot be used — apply again.
  • 🔄
    The E-Visa cannot be extended once issued. If you need to stay beyond the approved duration, you must exit Vietnam and re-apply from outside the country.
  • 👤
    You may apply on behalf of someone else using their personal information and passport copy, as long as all details are accurate.
  • 💳
    The visa fee is non-refundable in all circumstances, including if your application is rejected, if you make an error, or if you cancel your trip.
  • 🛂
    Check whether your nationality qualifies for a visa exemption with Vietnam before applying. If exempt, you may enter without an E-Visa for 30 or 45 days depending on your country's bilateral agreement.
  • 🚫
    Avoid third-party visa agencies advertising on search engines. Many charge inflated fees for the same result. The official portal at evisa.gov.vn is the only authorised channel for individual applications.
